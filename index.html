How a service runs

From market to table in under a minute.

Fully scheduled. No human in the loop until the brief lands in chat.

  1. 08:00

    Forager sources

    Deterministic pull from the RemoteOK API and the Hacker News “Who is hiring?” thread. Regex prefilter for AI/agent roles.

  2. 08:00

    Forager judges

    An LLM scores each candidate for fit against the career goal — with JSON repair and retry when it returns prose instead of data.

  3. 08:01

    Expediter verifies

    Every surviving URL is fetched live. Dead links are rejected, duplicates against the seen-log are dropped.

  4. 08:01

    Line Cook writes

    Fit notes, caveats, a skill-gap table, and “also worth a look.” The brief is filed and archived.

  5. 08:08

    Brief delivered

    The new brief lands in chat — automatically, three mornings a week. Failures page the owner; there’s a one-command kill switch.
